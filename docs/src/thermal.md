# Thermal detection (Phase 13)

People are warm against cool ground, so a thermal camera can show a person a colour camera misses, at night and in poor light. This phase trains the version 2 detector's architecture (D-FINE N at 640) on thermal drone images, scores it on days it never saw, and measures how well detectors carry over between colour and thermal. Design: D-751 in [decisions.md](decisions.md).

## Data

HIT-UAV (Suo et al., *Scientific Data* 10, 227, 2023; CC BY 4.0, D-748): 2,898 thermal images, 640 x 512, from a DJI Zenmuse XT2 (FLIR longwave sensor, 25 mm lens) on a DJI Matrice M210 V2, flown at 60 to 130 m with the camera 30 to 90 degrees down, by day and night, over schools, car parks, roads and playgrounds. Each file name records the time of day, altitude and camera angle, so results can be broken down by all three.

Classes are mapped to the version 2 detector's own (VisDrone numbers), so its output and evaluation groups are unchanged: Person to pedestrian, Car to car, Bicycle to bicycle (in neither group). DontCare (objects the annotators could not identify, possibly people) and OtherVehicle (not defined further) are ignored regions: detections on them neither count for nor against.

## Split

The dataset's own split divides images 70 / 10 / 20 at random. But the images are frames from videos, and most test images sit one to four frames from a training image of the same scene: a test there measures recognising scenes already seen. So the main split keeps whole capture days apart (9 days; validation 23 January 2021, test 16 and 21 January 2021, together 23 % of images). The test days cover every altitude, day and night. The official split is still used once, to compare with published results and to show what the shared videos add (`results/hituav/splits.json`, `scripts/hituav_coco_package.py`).

## Training

On a Kaggle GPU (`gpu/train_dfine_hituav_kaggle.py`, docs/gpu_training.md), with the colour detector's recipe (batch 8, the best validation epoch kept) and its number of training steps (D-757). HIT-UAV's training set is a third the size of VisDrone's, so this means 164 epochs, not 50. A first 50-epoch run stopped while still improving, and at the evaluation's score floor it found only {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_coco_e50_day_val.json#groups.person.ap50.max_recall|.0%}} of people on validation (person AP50 {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_coco_e50_day_val.json#groups.person.ap50.ap|.3f}}). Three runs: from the authors' COCO weights (the thermal detector), from the VisDrone-trained colour detector (does colour aerial training help?), and from COCO weights on the official split (comparison only). The two runs on the split by capture day came back complete (164 epochs each, about 5 hours on a T4); the official-split run was trained on Google Colab with the same script after the Kaggle GPU quota ran out, in two sessions, the second resuming from the first's saved state (158 epochs; its ONNX export also matches the training library, {{RESULT:edge/parity_dfine_hituav_official_coco.json#detections_onnx|,d}} detections).

On the validation day (not a test), at the evaluation's score floor, the retrained thermal detector finds {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_coco_day_val.json#groups.person.ap50.max_recall|.0%}} of people (person AP50 {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_coco_day_val.json#groups.person.ap50.ap|.3f}}, vehicles {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_coco_day_val.json#groups.vehicle.ap50.ap|.3f}}); the one started from the colour detector finds {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_visdrone_day_val.json#groups.person.ap50.max_recall|.0%}} (person AP50 {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_visdrone_day_val.json#groups.person.ap50.ap|.3f}}, vehicles {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_visdrone_day_val.json#groups.vehicle.ap50.ap|.3f}}). Validation is one day of {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_coco_day_val.json#n_images|,d}} images with {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_coco_day_val.json#groups.person.ap50.n_gt|,d}} people, so these numbers only show that training worked; the comparison between the two is for the test days. Both export to ONNX with the same detections as the training library on thermal frames ({{RESULT:edge/parity_dfine_hituav_day_coco.json#detections_onnx|,d}} and {{RESULT:edge/parity_dfine_hituav_day_visdrone.json#detections_onnx|,d}} detections, boxes within {{RESULT:edge/parity_dfine_hituav_day_visdrone.json#max_box_diff_px|.4f}} pixels).

## Thermal tracking: BIRDSAI

HIT-UAV holds single images, so tracking needs another dataset. BIRDSAI (Bondi et al., WACV 2020; LILA "Conservation Drones", Community Data License Agreement, permissive; D-754) holds 48 real thermal videos from fixed-wing drone patrols at night over African protected areas, at 60 to 120 m, with people and animals tracked frame by frame. Its cameras differ from HIT-UAV's (FLIR Vue Pro 640 or Tamarisk 640, 640 x 480, 19 mm lens, automatic gain), so it also tests whether the thermal detector carries over to another thermal camera and setting.

- 32 training videos (6 with people) tune the tracker's thresholds for people; 16 test videos (5 with people, 30 people tracked over 22,000 boxes) are scored once.
- Animals are not ignored: a person detection on an animal is a false alarm, as it would be for a search crew.
- People in these videos are small, typically 10 to 45 pixels tall.

```bash
python scripts/birdsai_prep.py
bash scripts/queues/phase13_birdsai.sh tune
bash scripts/queues/phase13_birdsai.sh test   # once
```

## Results

Every Phase 13 test was scored once, after the Phase 13 freeze. AP50 counts a detection as correct when it overlaps a labelled box by half; the intervals are 95 % bootstrap intervals that resample whole video settings (time of day, altitude and camera angle), not single images, because images of one setting are near-copies.

**Trained on thermal images, the detector finds most people on days it never saw.** On the two test days ({{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_coco_day_test.json#n_images|,d}} images, {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_coco_day_test.json#groups.person.ap50.n_gt|,d}} people, {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_coco_day_test.json#groups.vehicle.ap50.n_gt|,d}} vehicles), the thermal detector reaches person AP50 {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_coco_day_test.json#groups.person.ap50.ap|.3f}} (interval {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_coco_day_test.json#groups.person.ap50_ci95_resample_settings.0|.2f}} to {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_coco_day_test.json#groups.person.ap50_ci95_resample_settings.1|.2f}}) and finds {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_coco_day_test.json#groups.person.ap50.max_recall|.0%}} of people; vehicles {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_coco_day_test.json#groups.vehicle.ap50.ap|.3f}}. Every person in the test images is under 32 pixels tall: AP50 is {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_coco_day_test.json#groups.person.by_box_height_px.0-16.ap|.3f}} under 16 pixels and {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_coco_day_test.json#groups.person.by_box_height_px.16-32.ap|.3f}} from 16 to 32. It works at night as well as by day ({{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_coco_day_test.json#groups.person.by_time.night.ap|.3f}} against {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_coco_day_test.json#groups.person.by_time.day.ap|.3f}}), which is the point of a thermal camera. Started from the colour detector instead of general-purpose (COCO) weights, it scores {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_visdrone_day_test.json#groups.person.ap50.ap|.3f}} for people (interval {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_visdrone_day_test.json#groups.person.ap50_ci95_resample_settings.0|.2f}} to {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_visdrone_day_test.json#groups.person.ap50_ci95_resample_settings.1|.2f}}) and {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_visdrone_day_test.json#groups.vehicle.ap50.ap|.3f}} for vehicles. The intervals overlap widely, so this test does not show that colour aerial training helps.

The validation day had suggested far lower scores (person AP50 {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_coco_day_val.json#groups.person.ap50.ap|.3f}}). That day was flown only at 120 and 130 m and holds {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_day_coco_day_val.json#groups.person.ap50.n_gt|,d}} people (D-751), so it was a narrow and hard check; it was used only to pick the training epoch, and the test days are the result.

**Neither detector carries people across to the other kind of camera.** The colour detector on the thermal test days scores person AP50 {{RESULT:thermal/detect_visdrone_dfine_n_640_day_test.json#groups.person.ap50.ap|.3f}} (interval {{RESULT:thermal/detect_visdrone_dfine_n_640_day_test.json#groups.person.ap50_ci95_resample_settings.0|.2f}} to {{RESULT:thermal/detect_visdrone_dfine_n_640_day_test.json#groups.person.ap50_ci95_resample_settings.1|.2f}}), though vehicles carry over ({{RESULT:thermal/detect_visdrone_dfine_n_640_day_test.json#groups.vehicle.ap50.ap|.3f}}). The other way, the thermal detector on colour video (VisDrone MOT validation) scores {{RESULT:visdrone/val/detect_dfine_hituav_day_coco.json#results.person.ap50.ap|.3f}} for people and {{RESULT:visdrone/val/detect_dfine_hituav_day_coco.json#results.vehicle.ap50.ap|.3f}} for vehicles, against {{RESULT:visdrone/val/detect_dfine_n_640.json#results.person.ap50.ap|.3f}} and {{RESULT:visdrone/val/detect_dfine_n_640.json#results.vehicle.ap50.ap|.3f}} for the colour detector on the same video. A drone with both cameras needs both detectors.

**The dataset's own split flatters.** On the official split, whose test images are mostly neighbouring frames of training videos (D-751), the same recipe scores person AP50 {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_official_coco_official_test.json#groups.person.ap50.ap|.3f}} (interval {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_official_coco_official_test.json#groups.person.ap50_ci95_resample_settings.0|.2f}} to {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_official_coco_official_test.json#groups.person.ap50_ci95_resample_settings.1|.2f}}) and vehicles {{RESULT:thermal/detect_dfine_hituav_official_coco_official_test.json#groups.vehicle.ap50.ap|.3f}}. That measures recognising scenes already seen. The split by capture day gives the figure to expect on new days.

**The colour detector barely finds people in thermal video.** The version 2 colour detector (D-FINE N, trained on VisDrone) was run on BIRDSAI's 32 training videos, and the tracker's thresholds for people were tuned by the D-733 rule. At the chosen thresholds (high {{RESULT:birdsai/train/grid_dfine_n_640__motion__person.json#selected.track_high_thresh|.2f}}, new {{RESULT:birdsai/train/grid_dfine_n_640__motion__person.json#selected.new_track_thresh|.2f}}), tracks covered {{RESULT:birdsai/train/grid_dfine_n_640__motion__person.json#points.14.metrics.person.OVERALL.recall|.1%}} of the person boxes, and {{RESULT:birdsai/train/grid_dfine_n_640__motion__person.json#points.14.metrics.person.OVERALL.precision|.1%}} of person-track boxes were people. That is {{RESULT:birdsai/train/grid_dfine_n_640__motion__person.json#points.14.metrics.person.OVERALL.num_false_positives|,.0f}} false person boxes against {{RESULT:birdsai/train/grid_dfine_n_640__motion__person.json#points.14.metrics.person.OVERALL.num_objects|,.0f}} true ones, with person IDF1 {{RESULT:birdsai/train/grid_dfine_n_640__motion__person.json#points.14.metrics.person.OVERALL.idf1|.3f}}. What looks like a person in colour does not carry over to thermal. The likely reason, not tested here, is that a warm body shows as a bright blob, without the shape, shading or colour the detector learned. So a search drone with only a thermal camera needs a detector trained on thermal images.

**The thermal detector finds far more, but not enough.** The retrained thermal detector (from COCO weights, on HIT-UAV's training days) was tuned the same way on the same 32 training videos (D-768). At its chosen thresholds (high {{RESULT:birdsai/train/grid_dfine_hituav_day_coco__motion__person.json#selected.track_high_thresh|.2f}}, new {{RESULT:birdsai/train/grid_dfine_hituav_day_coco__motion__person.json#selected.new_track_thresh|.2f}}), tracks covered {{RESULT:birdsai/train/grid_dfine_hituav_day_coco__motion__person.json#points.33.metrics.person.OVERALL.recall|.1%}} of the person boxes, and {{RESULT:birdsai/train/grid_dfine_hituav_day_coco__motion__person.json#points.33.metrics.person.OVERALL.precision|.1%}} of person-track boxes were people ({{RESULT:birdsai/train/grid_dfine_hituav_day_coco__motion__person.json#points.33.metrics.person.OVERALL.num_false_positives|,.0f}} false person boxes), with person IDF1 {{RESULT:birdsai/train/grid_dfine_hituav_day_coco__motion__person.json#points.33.metrics.person.OVERALL.idf1|.3f}}. It depends on size: of person boxes 32 to 64 pixels tall, tracks covered {{RESULT:birdsai/train/grid_dfine_hituav_day_coco__motion__person.json#points.33.by_height.person.32-64.matched|,d}} of {{RESULT:birdsai/train/grid_dfine_hituav_day_coco__motion__person.json#points.33.by_height.person.32-64.gt_object_frames|,d}}; of those under 16 pixels, {{RESULT:birdsai/train/grid_dfine_hituav_day_coco__motion__person.json#points.33.by_height.person.0-16.matched|,d}} of {{RESULT:birdsai/train/grid_dfine_hituav_day_coco__motion__person.json#points.33.by_height.person.0-16.gt_object_frames|,d}}. Thermal training carries over to another thermal camera much better than colour training does, but most of what it reports as people in these night patrols over wildlife areas is not a person. These are tuning results on training videos.

**The one-time test agrees: thermal training carries over, but not far enough.** After the Phase 13 freeze, both detectors ran once on BIRDSAI's 16 test videos at the thresholds chosen on the training videos (D-774). Frame by frame, person AP50 on every tenth test frame was {{RESULT:birdsai/test/detect_dfine_hituav_day_coco.json#results.person.ap50.ap|.3f}} for the thermal detector against {{RESULT:birdsai/test/detect_dfine_n_640.json#results.person.ap50.ap|.3f}} for the colour detector. ## Limits

- **One sensor.** One camera model at one resolution (640 x 512) and focal length; another sensor, lens or resolution changes how many pixels a person covers at a given height.
- **Ground temperature.** Contrast depends on the difference between a body and its surroundings. Sun-warmed ground, warm roads and buildings reduce it; cold nights increase it. Results are reported by day and night, but the dataset records no temperatures, so the effect cannot be measured directly.
- **Places and seasons.** A limited set of sites (schools, car parks, roads, playgrounds), flown on nine days in December 2020 and January 2021. Forest, open country, water and summer heat are not covered.
- **Tracking on another dataset.** HIT-UAV has single images, not tracks, so thermal tracking is scored on BIRDSAI, with different cameras, places and targets (night patrols over wildlife areas).
