# Hypotheses H6 to H16: outcomes

Version 2 set out seven hypotheses before the work began (build2 Section 14), and Phase 16 added four more before its runs (H13 to H16, D-764), each with the test that would falsify it. Each is reported here as supported, partly supported, not supported or not yet tested, with the evidence. Every number is filled from a results file.

## H6: the box-to-ground step causes most of the extra range error on detected objects

*Hypothesis:* end-to-end range error on detected objects is larger than ground-pixel range error mainly because of the box-to-ground step, and the Phase 9 footprint fix removes most of the difference.

**Supported, for vehicles.** With exact poses (SynDrone), ranging a ground pixel is exact, so any systematic error on a detected vehicle comes from choosing its ground point. Ranging the bottom edge of the box read vehicles short: median signed error {{RESULT:syndrone/development/eval_v2trk_d735.json#vehicle.pooled.range_rel_err_median_signed|+.1%}} pooled, {{RESULT:syndrone/development/eval_v2trk_d735.json#vehicle.by_height.h20.range_rel_err_median_signed|+.1%}} at 20 m (development towns, version 1's tracks). Ranging to the footprint centre (D-736) and then to the contact point (D-743) brought it to {{RESULT:syndrone/development/eval_v1_acc_contact.json#vehicle.pooled.range_rel_err_median_signed|+.1%}} ({{RESULT:syndrone/development/eval_v1_acc_contact.json#vehicle.by_height.h20.range_rel_err_median_signed|+.1%}} at 20 m), the median absolute error from {{RESULT:syndrone/development/eval_v2trk_d735.json#vehicle.pooled.range_rel_err_median_abs|.1%}} to {{RESULT:syndrone/development/eval_v1_acc_contact.json#vehicle.pooled.range_rel_err_median_abs|.1%}}, and the 90th percentile from {{RESULT:syndrone/development/eval_v2trk_d735.json#vehicle.pooled.range_rel_err_p90_abs|.1%}} to {{RESULT:syndrone/development/eval_v1_acc_contact.json#vehicle.pooled.range_rel_err_p90_abs|.1%}}. The contact point was fitted on these towns, so its step was tested on the calibration towns: median absolute error {{RESULT:syndrone/calibration/eval_v1_acc_base.json#vehicle.pooled.range_rel_err_median_abs|.1%}} to {{RESULT:syndrone/calibration/eval_v1_acc_contact.json#vehicle.pooled.range_rel_err_median_abs|.1%}} with version 1's detector, {{RESULT:syndrone/calibration/eval_v2_dfine.json#vehicle.pooled.range_rel_err_median_abs|.1%}} to {{RESULT:syndrone/calibration/eval_v2_dfine_contact.json#vehicle.pooled.range_rel_err_median_abs|.1%}} with version 2's. On the test towns, run once, vehicles' median error is {{RESULT:syndrone/test/eval_p10_v2_cal.json#vehicle.pooled.range_rel_err_median_abs|.1%}}. People needed no correction: a standing person's box already ends at the feet, and moving it made them worse (D-736). Not tested: real footage with known distances, where pose error, not the box, is likely to dominate.

## H7: speeds shown only when the filter is confident are accurate and calibrated

*Hypothesis:* speeds shown only when the filter is confident are accurate to the stated tolerance, and their intervals are calibrated.

**Not supported.** No display rule reached the target (9 in 10 shown speeds within tolerance, at least 100 shown) on the development towns with the version 2 detector: the best rule for people was right {{RESULT:syndrone/development/speed_display_fit_v2_dfine_cal__person__nis_upper.json#most_accurate.accuracy|.0%}} of the time, for vehicles {{RESULT:syndrone/development/speed_display_fit_v2_dfine_cal__vehicle__tol1.3889_0.05__nis_upper.json#most_accurate.accuracy|.0%}} (D-739, D-740). So, as fixed before the test, the pipeline shows no speeds, and the one-time test could not score displayed speeds. The intervals themselves were calibrated (conformal, D-737), but a calibrated interval does not single out the speeds that are wrong. With version 1's detector a person rule came close ({{RESULT:syndrone/development/speed_display_fit_v2trk_final_cal__person.json#most_accurate.accuracy|.0%}}, D-739), so a more precise person detector is the likeliest route.

## H8: conditional calibration brings each group's coverage close to nominal

*Hypothesis:* conditional calibration brings coverage within each group to within a few points of nominal, where the single scalar did not.

**Partly supported.** On the SynDrone test towns, calibrated on other towns and run once, range intervals at nominal 68 / 90 / 95 % held {{RESULT:syndrone/test/eval_p10_v2_cal.json#person.pooled.range_calibrated_coverage_0.68|.0%}} / {{RESULT:syndrone/test/eval_p10_v2_cal.json#person.pooled.range_calibrated_coverage_0.90|.0%}} / {{RESULT:syndrone/test/eval_p10_v2_cal.json#person.pooled.range_calibrated_coverage_0.95|.0%}} for people and {{RESULT:syndrone/test/eval_p10_v2_cal.json#vehicle.pooled.range_calibrated_coverage_0.68|.0%}} / {{RESULT:syndrone/test/eval_p10_v2_cal.json#vehicle.pooled.range_calibrated_coverage_0.90|.0%}} / {{RESULT:syndrone/test/eval_p10_v2_cal.json#vehicle.pooled.range_calibrated_coverage_0.95|.0%}} for vehicles: within a few points at 90 and 95 %, about ten points too wide at 68 %. Leave-one-flight-out, the conditional (Mondrian) method beat version 1's single factor clearly, but still missed nominal by 8 to 10 points on average per cell and held-out flight (D-737): flights differ more than any grouping by angle, range and class captures. On Mid-Air, with fused poses, the filter's own covariance gave {{RESULT:midair/test/navigation/ranging_msckf_final.json#conditions.fused.terrain.coverage_raw.0.68|.0%}} / {{RESULT:midair/test/navigation/ranging_msckf_final.json#conditions.fused.terrain.coverage_raw.0.90|.0%}} / {{RESULT:midair/test/navigation/ranging_msckf_final.json#conditions.fused.terrain.coverage_raw.0.95|.0%}} at the same levels. The SynDrone calibration holds for exact poses only; with navigation errors added the 90 % intervals held far less (docs/syndrone_eval.md), so they must be refitted for the navigation flown.

## H9: camera, IMU and GPS fusion brings range error close to the exact-pose result

*Hypothesis:* tightly coupled camera, IMU and GPS fusion brings ranging error with realistic navigation close to the exact-pose result.

**Supported.** On the Mid-Air test flights, run once, median range error was {{RESULT:midair/test/navigation/ranging_msckf_final.json#conditions.fused.terrain.median|.1%}} with fused poses against {{RESULT:midair/test/navigation/ranging_msckf_final.json#conditions.exact.terrain.median|.1%}} with exact poses, and {{RESULT:midair/test/navigation/ranging_msckf_final.json#conditions.nav_good.terrain.median|.1%}} / {{RESULT:midair/test/navigation/ranging_msckf_final.json#conditions.nav_typical.terrain.median|.1%}} / {{RESULT:midair/test/navigation/ranging_msckf_final.json#conditions.nav_poor.terrain.median|.1%}} under version 1's assumed good, typical and poor navigation (D-741). Fusion recovers most of the gap between assumed typical navigation and exact poses. The filter was not fully consistent: its normalised error for all states was above the expected band, driven by a GPS burst the filter took in ungated; a GPS gate was added afterwards and checked on development flights only (D-744). Mid-Air flights are synthetic, so real sensors may do worse.

## H10: a permissive detector and the project's tracker match or beat the Ultralytics stack

*Hypothesis:* a permissively licensed detector and our own tracker match or beat the Ultralytics stack on VisDrone.

**Partly supported.** On VisDrone test-dev, run once, identity F1 was {{RESULT:headline.json#v2.test_vehicle_idf1|.3f}} for vehicles and {{RESULT:headline.json#v2.test_person_idf1|.3f}} for people, against {{RESULT:visdrone/test-dev/B2_yolo26n_960_tuned_botsort_cmc.json#metrics.vehicle.OVERALL.idf1|.3f}} and {{RESULT:visdrone/test-dev/B2_yolo26n_960_tuned_botsort_cmc.json#metrics.person.OVERALL.idf1|.3f}} for version 1 (which ran at 960 pixels; D-730). It does not match on tracking. It does beat version 1 on small-object detection: {{RESULT:headline.json#v2.test_person_det_recall_16_32|.0%}} of people 16 to 32 pixels tall detected, against {{RESULT:headline.json#v2.test_v1_person_det_recall_16_32|.0%}}; but only {{RESULT:headline.json#v2.test_person_recall_16_32|.0%}} are kept by a track, because the tracker starts tracks only at high scores, which small people rarely reach (D-730, D-733). The licence goal is met: no AGPL code runs in version 2 (D-725, D-732).

## H11: the pipeline fits a Raspberry Pi 5 at a useful rate for search

*Hypothesis:* the pipeline fits a Raspberry Pi 5 at a useful rate for search.

**Not yet tested.** No Pi 5 has been available, so the detector's time on one is estimated, not measured: about {{RESULT:headline.json#v2.val_dfine_n_640_pi_ms|,.0f}} ms per frame for D-FINE N at 640 (0.75 to 1.5 times that, D-723), so detection about every 0.3 s. The rate a search needs was left to be set from the search pattern's speed and image overlap; the Phase 14 measurement now bears on it: at SynDrone's 21.9 m/s and 25 frames per second, a person's chance of being detected in a pass rose steeply with the number of frames they stayed in view (docs/multi_drone.md), so detecting on fewer frames is expected to cost detections, not only latency. `make pi-benchmark` gives the measurement when a device exists.

## H12: sharing observations between drones speeds up search and degrades gracefully

*Hypothesis:* sharing observations between drones reduces time to find and repeated coverage compared with independent search, and degrades gracefully with packet loss.

**Partly supported.** In the Phase 14 simulation, run once on test seeds with the camera measured on SynDrone, sharing beat the same planner without sharing at every team size (four drones: mean time to find {{RESULT:multi_drone/test_p10_v2_cal.json#results.chosen_share_n4.restricted_mean_time_s|,.0f}} s against {{RESULT:multi_drone/test_p10_v2_cal.json#results.chosen_noshare_n4.restricted_mean_time_s|,.0f}} s), repeated slightly less ground ({{RESULT:multi_drone/test_p10_v2_cal.json#results.chosen_share_n4.repeated_coverage|.0%}} of passes against {{RESULT:multi_drone/test_p10_v2_cal.json#results.chosen_noshare_n4.repeated_coverage|.0%}}), and degraded gracefully: at 90 % packet loss it fell back to no-sharing time ({{RESULT:multi_drone/test_p10_v2_cal.json#results.chosen_share_loss0.9.restricted_mean_time_s|,.0f}} s), never below it. With too little bandwidth it did not degrade gracefully: below the bytes sharing produces, its advantage soon disappeared (development seeds, after a fault in the test's budget model was found; D-756). Against the other baseline, independent lawnmower strips, it lost at every team size (four drones: lawnmower {{RESULT:multi_drone/test_p10_v2_cal.json#results.lawnmower_n4.restricted_mean_time_s|,.0f}} s), because the detector's false alarms pull map-guided drones back to check them. Details and the byte-budget follow-up: [docs/multi_drone.md](multi_drone.md).

## H13: sweeping first, then looking again, confirms a find sooner than the lawnmower

*Hypothesis:* sweep first then look again reaches a confirmed find sooner than the lawnmower, with no more crew alarms before it. *Test:* Phase 16 test seeds, paired.

Crew alarms before the find were not clearly different ({{RESULT:multi_drone/phase16_paired_test.json#comparisons.H13_sweep_minus_lawnmower_n4.crew.mean|+,.0f}} ({{RESULT:multi_drone/phase16_paired_test.json#comparisons.H13_sweep_minus_lawnmower_n4.crew.ci95.0|+,.0f}} to {{RESULT:multi_drone/phase16_paired_test.json#comparisons.H13_sweep_minus_lawnmower_n4.crew.ci95.1|+,.0f}}) at four drones). ## H14: compact sharing keeps the result with far fewer bytes

*Hypothesis:* compact sharing matches the Phase 14 encoding's time to confirmed find with fewer bytes, and keeps it at Phase 14's byte budgets. *Test:* Phase 16 test seeds, paired.

**Supported.** On the same planner (the Phase 14 auction) compact sharing used {{RESULT:multi_drone/phase16_test.json#results.auction14_compact_n4.bytes_per_drone_s_mean|,.1f}} bytes per drone per second against {{RESULT:multi_drone/phase16_test.json#results.auction14_share_n4.bytes_per_drone_s_mean|,.0f}}, with no measurable change in time ({{RESULT:multi_drone/phase16_paired_test.json#comparisons.H14_auction_compact_minus_frozen.time.mean|+,.0f}} ({{RESULT:multi_drone/phase16_paired_test.json#comparisons.H14_auction_compact_minus_frozen.time.ci95.0|+,.0f}} to {{RESULT:multi_drone/phase16_paired_test.json#comparisons.H14_auction_compact_minus_frozen.time.ci95.1|+,.0f}}) s). The sweep with compact sharing uses {{RESULT:multi_drone/phase16_test.json#results.sweep_n4.bytes_per_drone_s_mean|,.1f}} bytes per drone per second, and every Phase 14 budget, down to 52, left its time unchanged ({{RESULT:multi_drone/phase16_paired_test.json#comparisons.H14_sweep_budget52_minus_unlimited.time.mean|+,.0f}} ({{RESULT:multi_drone/phase16_paired_test.json#comparisons.H14_sweep_budget52_minus_unlimited.time.ci95.0|+,.0f}} to {{RESULT:multi_drone/phase16_paired_test.json#comparisons.H14_sweep_budget52_minus_unlimited.time.ci95.1|+,.0f}}) s at 52). ## H16: a coordinator drone helps, and losing it costs no more than losing any drone

*Hypothesis:* a coordinator drone reaches confirmed finds sooner than leaderless, and losing it costs no more than losing any other drone. *Test:* Phase 16 test seeds, paired.

**Partly supported.** Losing the coordinator cost no more than losing any other drone ({{RESULT:multi_drone/phase16_paired_test.json#comparisons.H16_lose_coordinator_minus_lose_any.time.mean|+,.0f}} ({{RESULT:multi_drone/phase16_paired_test.json#comparisons.H16_lose_coordinator_minus_lose_any.time.ci95.0|+,.0f}} to {{RESULT:multi_drone/phase16_paired_test.json#comparisons.H16_lose_coordinator_minus_lose_any.time.ci95.1|+,.0f}}) s), because the others fall back to the leaderless rules. But the coordinator was not faster with the primary camera at 2, 4 or 8 drones ({{RESULT:multi_drone/phase16_paired_test.json#comparisons.H16_coord_minus_sweep_n4.time.mean|+,.0f}} ({{RESULT:multi_drone/phase16_paired_test.json#comparisons.H16_coord_minus_sweep_n4.time.ci95.0|+,.0f}} to {{RESULT:multi_drone/phase16_paired_test.json#comparisons.H16_coord_minus_sweep_n4.time.ci95.1|+,.0f}}) s at four). It was faster only with the rural camera ({{RESULT:multi_drone/phase16_paired_test.json#comparisons.H16_rural_coord_minus_sweep.time.mean|+,.0f}} ({{RESULT:multi_drone/phase16_paired_test.json#comparisons.H16_rural_coord_minus_sweep.time.ci95.0|+,.0f}} to {{RESULT:multi_drone/phase16_paired_test.json#comparisons.H16_rural_coord_minus_sweep.time.ci95.1|+,.0f}}) s), one comparison of four, so the evidence that it helps is weak.
